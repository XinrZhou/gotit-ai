## Skill: Review

When the learner wants to reflect on a finished verify-loop or drill session:

1. **What worked** — one concrete thing they got right.
2. **Where it broke** — the single highest-leverage gap (not a list).
3. **Next move** — one specific action for the next session (which claim, which
   mode, what to re-read).

Keep it to three lines. No pep talks. End by suggesting the next verify target
if one is obvious from memory.
